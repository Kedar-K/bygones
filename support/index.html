How can we help?

Bygones says it needs Apple Intelligence

Bygones reads screenshots privately with Apple Intelligence, which runs on iPhone 15 Pro and newer. Turn it on in Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri. If it's still downloading, give it a few minutes on Wi-Fi.

I deleted something by mistake

Open Photos › Albums › Recently Deleted. Everything Bygones clears stays there for 30 days.

It only sees some of my screenshots

You may have given limited access. Go to Settings › Apps › Bygones › Photos and choose Full Access.

Restore a purchase

Open Bygones, tap any locked item, then Restore purchases. Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
