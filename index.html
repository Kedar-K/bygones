Questions

Good to know

Which iPhones does Bygones work on?

Any iPhone with Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or newer), with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings.

Are my screenshots uploaded anywhere?

No. Bygones reads them on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence. Nothing is uploaded, there's no account, and there's no tracking or ad analytics.

What does it read?

Only your Screenshots album: the text and layout of each screenshot, so it can tell a boarding pass from a recipe.

Will it delete anything on its own?

Never. You review every suggestion. Cleared screenshots go to Recently Deleted in Photos and stay there for 30 days.
