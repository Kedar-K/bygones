Privacy policy

Privacy, in plain words.

Last updated 13 October 2026.

The short version

Bygones does not collect, upload, store, sell or share any of your data. Your screenshots never leave your iPhone.

Screenshots uploadedNever
AccountsNone
Analytics or trackersNone
Sold to anyoneNever

What Bygones reads

With your permission, Bygones reads the screenshots in your Photos library: the text and layout of each one, so it can tell a boarding pass from a receipt. It does not need or use your other photos.

Where that happens

Entirely on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device text recognition and Apple Intelligence. No server is involved, and we never see your screenshots.

What stays on your phone

A small private index of what has been read is kept inside the app on your device, so Bygones does not have to re-read everything. It is removed when you delete the app.

Deleting screenshots

Bygones only deletes screenshots you confirm. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in Photos, where they stay for 30 days.

Calendar and notifications

If you choose to add an event, Bygones opens the system calendar editor with the details filled in. It cannot read your calendar. Optional weekly reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

Bygones does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here. Questions: open a support request.
